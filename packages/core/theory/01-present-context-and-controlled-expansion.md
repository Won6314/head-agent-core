# Present Context and Controlled Expansion

An LLM acts from the context assembled for the present interaction, not from durable human-like memory. This is an operational model, not a literal claim about model cognition. It changes a common intuition: putting more material in front of a model does not automatically preserve the right facts or improve the next decision. Context can contain obsolete direction, an unmarked guess, or competing sources; the model still has to act from what is present and interpretable now. Its quality therefore depends on authority, relevance, timing, and ownership—not volume alone.

The risk grows when output is allowed to supply its own next premise. Consider a public-safe planning task: a request names a required audience and constraint, but a first draft leaves the constraint out. A later draft treats the first as the brief; a review then checks polish and consistency against that narrowed brief. Each result may look coherent, yet the omitted constraint has moved from an omission to an assumed scope. The same pattern can turn a guess into a requirement, an incomplete account into a decision, or a confident report into a reason to expand further. Detail and plausibility do not demonstrate that the intended target survived.

HEAD responds by treating a coherent expansion as a boundary: begin from an authoritative premise, produce one bounded result, and inspect evidence appropriate to that result before it shapes later work. The check is not a ritual of distrusting all generation. It makes the consequential transition visible: a source comparison might test a factual claim, while a review against the stated direction might test whether a proposed result retained scope. Evidence can show that the result needs correction as well as that it is ready to support the next step.

This response can still fail. The premise may already be wrong, the check may use the wrong source or test the wrong claim, and low-consequence work may not justify a formal gate. Nor does it forbid multi-step reasoning. It says that when generated work would become authority for later work, the expansion and its evidence should be proportionate and observable. That raises the next question: who is entitled to choose the premise, the evidence, and the next result? [Ownership and context boundaries](02-ownership-and-context-boundaries.md) answers it.

# Continuity and Canon

Ownership boundaries solve only the present-context problem. Durable work also crosses interruptions, compaction, and handoffs. The tempting response is to treat the latest summary, progress record, history, runtime state, or worker report as the current truth. But each is a representation of work, not the work agreement itself. In being shortened or reframed, it can omit a condition, merge two decisions, or narrow an unresolved question.

For example, a work agreement may require two outcomes to fit together. After interruption, a concise summary records that the first is complete and names only the second's most visible requirement. A new owner can make real progress from that summary, then verify the result against the reduced requirement. The work appears to recover successfully, but the relationship between the outcomes has disappeared from the target. Validation has not failed mechanically; its oracle has been silently narrowed.

HEAD responds with canon: a durable record outside the lossy summary that preserves purpose, structure, decisions, current position, and the next result across interruption and compaction. The durable User–HEAD agreement outranks subordinate retrieval records. Summaries and reports remain useful for orientation and for finding supporting evidence, but they cannot silently redefine agreed scope. A stable canonical target lets a later owner compare a current representation against the agreement rather than mistaking the representation for the agreement.

Canon has limits. It can preserve a mistaken agreement, retained sources can be inaccurate, and a canonical record can become stale when the User changes direction. More records are not automatically more continuity; duplicated or ambiguous records can create another authority conflict. Canon preserves decision authority and continuity so correction remains possible against a stable target; it does not establish reality or replace judgment. [Evolution, evidence, and limits](04-evolution-evidence-and-limits.md) explains why this response remains revisable and bounded.

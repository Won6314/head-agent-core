# Contract A Prime

## Trigger

The user says exactly `정리 끝` after rich planning `P` based on `a`.

## Exact reads

Read immutable `a`, the rich planning conversation since `a`, the exact accepted and rejected user decisions in that conversation, and `anchor-operations.md`. Do not pass the raw planning conversation to the loaded child.

## Authority

The user owns material decisions. The current `P` compiles and judges the transfer. A loaded comprehension child may identify ambiguity but cannot decide it.

## Produced result

Perform this loop:

1. Compile one Plan Contract containing the accepted whole plan, locked decisions, material rejected interpretations and reasons, unresolved decisions, authority boundaries, success and stop conditions, return-to-user conditions, and material dependencies on `a`.
2. Load `a` in task mode without `save_as`. Give the child only the Plan Contract and ask it to restate the whole plan, locked and rejected meanings, unresolved matters, its authority, and return conditions.
3. Inspect the handoff. If it omits, alters, resolves, or invents a material decision, correct the Plan Contract or return to the user and repeat from step 2.
4. After the parent accepts the comprehension, load `a` again with only the exact accepted Plan Contract and save the clean child under the unique name selected for conceptual anchor `a′`. The comprehension handoff is validation evidence, not anchor content.
5. Verify the saved identity and keep `P` as the continuing lineage controller.

The result is clean immutable `a′`; it inherits `a` and the accepted Plan Contract, not `P`'s raw conversation.

## Mutation class

Plan Contract canon, disposable comprehension tasks, and one immutable accepted-anchor creation. No production mutation.
